## Registered question H13

Describe how the fixed O06-first, R02-second policy routes BRISC development validation, X02 OpenNeuro, and X03 UPENN-GBM cases, and quantify the segmentation quality and failure rate among cases that reach STANDARD display.

## Decision rule

Descriptive only. No threshold is optimized and no pass/fail gate applies. Results are reported independently by cohort with group-appropriate bootstrap intervals.

## Interpretation

The analysis may state the observed fraction of Dice-below-0.50 cases routed to review and the observed Dice among STANDARD cases. It cannot claim clinical protection, prospective safety, or generalization beyond the registered cohorts.
